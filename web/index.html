      Field guide: MetaMining

      How MetaMining surveys latent claims across parallel branches to find high-value veins.

      1. Branches, not single steps
      Every expedition runs several branches in parallel, each identified by branch and step (b2 s14). The claim map colors branches so diverging lines of inquiry stay visible.
      2. Novelty and coherence
      Each finding scores novelty (how far it sits from visited claims) and coherence (how well it holds together). Findings above the gold bar on both axes are flagged as gold veins.
      3. Topology radar
      Betti numbers track the shape of explored space: b1 loops mean a branch is circling old ground, b2 voids mark unexplored chambers worth pushing into. Highlighted cells need attention.
      4. Pareto frontier and sharing
      The frontier lists non-dominated finds: no other finding beats them on both novelty and coherence. Copy any entry raw or formatted, or take the whole gold thread when the run completes.
